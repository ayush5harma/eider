# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Assume good faith, disagree about the work and not the person,
and keep discussion on topic. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

**No piracy: do not request, link or share sources of decrypted App Store
apps. Eider does not decrypt anything.** Issues, pull requests, discussions
and examples that do so will be closed or removed, and repeat offences lead to
a ban.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
ban contributors who break these rules. Report a conduct problem privately
through the repository's Security tab (private vulnerability reporting), and
say it is a conduct report.
